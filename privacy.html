1. Information I may collect

When you contact me through this website, email, or phone, I may collect your name, email address, phone number, project details, and any other information you choose to share. I only collect what is needed to respond to your enquiry and provide the service requested.

2. How I use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or project request.
  • Discuss services such as SEO, website development, and digital marketing support.
  • Manage communication, quoting, and project planning.
  • Improve the quality and usefulness of the website experience.

3. Website analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to better understand how visitors use the site. Analytics data may include pages visited, time spent on the website, device/browser information, and referral sources. This helps improve content, usability, and performance.

Important: I do not sell personal data. I may use aggregated analytics insights to understand website traffic and behaviour, but I do not use this information for profiling or marketing purposes beyond improving the website experience.

4. Data sharing

I do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. If needed, I may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the website or manage communication, but only for the purpose of delivering the relevant service and in line with reasonable privacy practices.

5. Your rights

You can contact me at any time to ask for the information I hold about you, request a correction, or ask that your information not be used for a specific purpose. If you have a privacy concern, please reach out directly and I will do my best to address it promptly.
